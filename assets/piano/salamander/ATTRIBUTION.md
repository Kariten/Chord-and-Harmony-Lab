# Salamander Grand Piano

These samples are adapted from the Salamander Grand Piano V3 library,
recorded by Alexander Holm from a Yamaha C5 grand piano.

- Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
- Original project: https://sfzinstruments.github.io/pianos/salamander/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The web application uses the MP3 conversion published by the Tone.js audio
repository. Samples are stored at minor-third intervals from A0 through C8 and
are pitch-shifted at playback time to cover the full 88-key piano range.
